Profile

Reality Check is led by someone who has seen the system from the inside: as a veteran, as a survivor, and as a person who knows exactly what it feels like to be on the wrong side of closed doors.

Charlie, the cat who appears on the homepage, is not a mascot invented for branding. He is a real companion — a daily anchor who represents loyalty, comfort, and the quiet, steady presence that so many people living on the edge never get.

This profile is a promise: the stories behind Reality Check are real, and the work will always be shaped by compassion, honesty, and the urgency of those still waiting for help.
