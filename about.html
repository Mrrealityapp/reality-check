About Reality Check

Built from lived experience, Reality Check is a personal mission with a public purpose.

I’m a disabled veteran living on a fixed income, building Reality Check from my home with my cat, Charlie, beside me. I’ve seen how easy it is for scammers to weaponize “need” — and how that hurts the people and animals who truly need help.

Reality Check is my answer: a way to document the real situation, share proof, and invite support honestly. When you give, you’ll see where it goes. When someone is helped, you’ll see the update.

This isn’t a polished corporate charity. It’s a ground-level, transparent lifeline — built by someone who knows how it feels to need a lifeline too.
