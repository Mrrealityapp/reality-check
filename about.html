About Reality Check

Reality Check exists because too many people have been burned by broken promises, fake causes, and systems that look caring on the surface but fail when it matters most.

Our work is grounded in lived experience: the fear of not being believed, the exhaustion of navigating services alone, and the simple need for warmth, food, and safety.

We are building a platform where donors and those in need can trust that what they see is real — and that support actually reaches the people and animals it was meant for.
