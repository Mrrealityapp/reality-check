About Reality Check

Reality Check was born from lived experience — a blend of personal struggle, hard-earned resilience, and a mission to help others navigate a world where truth, safety, and trust are too often missing.

The Founder

My name is Chris — a disabled veteran living on a fixed income, rebuilding life one step at a time with my cat Charlie by my side. Radical transparency isn’t a branding choice; it’s survival. Every day is a balance of keeping a roof overhead, managing basic needs, and trying to build something that can help others who feel unseen or unsupported.

Charlie is more than a companion — he’s a lifeline. His presence is woven into this project because he’s part of the reason I keep pushing forward. Reality Check reflects that same spirit: persistence, honesty, and hope even when things are hard.

Why Reality Check Exists

The internet is full of noise, misinformation, fake profiles, and people who take advantage of others. Reality Check was created to push back — to give people a way to verify truth, protect themselves, and support those who genuinely need help.

This project is still growing, but the mission is clear: build a platform rooted in honesty, compassion, and real-world impact. A place where people can feel safe, informed, and connected to something real.

Where It's Going

Reality Check is evolving into a resource for people who need clarity, support, and a community that values truth. With time, stability, and the generosity of supporters, this project will expand into tools, verification systems, and outreach that help others facing hardship — just like I have.

Every visit, every share, every donation helps keep this mission alive and moving forward.
