Sustainability

Sustainability at Reality Check isn’t just about the environment — it’s about building a stable foundation for people, animals, and the mission itself. This page explains how the project stays alive, grows responsibly, and supports those who rely on it.

Personal Sustainability

Reality Check is built and maintained by a disabled veteran living on a fixed income. Stability is not a luxury — it’s a daily challenge. Sustainability here means ensuring basic needs are met: housing, food, medical care, and caring for Charlie, whose companionship is essential to this work.

Every bit of support helps maintain the foundation that allows Reality Check to exist at all.

Project Sustainability

As the platform grows, sustainability means building tools and resources that are reliable, transparent, and accessible. Reality Check is designed to evolve slowly and responsibly, without sacrificing honesty or overextending beyond what can be maintained.

Donations directly support development, hosting, outreach, and the time required to build something that genuinely helps people.

Community Sustainability

Reality Check aims to create a community built on truth, compassion, and mutual support. Sustainability here means uplifting people who are struggling, protecting those who are vulnerable, and ensuring that help reaches both humans and animals who need it most.

As the project grows, so will its ability to support others in meaningful, lasting ways.
