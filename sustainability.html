Sustainability & future plans

Reality Check is starting small, with a long-term vision to help more people and animals safely.

In the early phase, support goes directly to survival, stability, and basic infrastructure for the project: keeping housing secure, caring for Charlie, and building the tools and documentation that make transparency possible.

As the project stabilizes, I plan to document more stories, help verify others in need, and share simple frameworks that anyone can copy to build trust with their own supporters.
