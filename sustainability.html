Sustainability

Sustainability is not just about the environment; it is about building systems that do not collapse on the people who rely on them.

Reality Check is committed to sustainable operations — financially, technically, and ethically. That means clear budgets, realistic goals, and a focus on work that can last.

As we grow, this page will share how we design programs that are responsible to donors, the planet, and the communities we serve.
