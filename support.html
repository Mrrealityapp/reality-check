Support

If you are confused, frustrated, or unsure how to move forward, you are not alone. Reality Check exists partly because systems are often designed to be confusing.

As we grow, we will provide direct donor support, help interpreting impact information, and guidance for people trying to navigate services, shelters, and resources.

For now, this page is a placeholder — a promise that support will not be an afterthought. It will be central to how Reality Check operates.
