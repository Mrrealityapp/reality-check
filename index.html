Meet Charlie

Charlie is more than a cat — he’s the heartbeat of Reality Check, the quiet presence that kept me going when everything else fell apart.

His calm eyes and steady company turned long nights of doubt into something survivable when there was no safety net left.

When you see Charlie on this page, you’re seeing the reminder that someone out there still cares and still shows up.

Reality Check is built to be that same steady presence for people who feel forgotten and worn down by the world.

Me & Mama

My mother taught me compassion the hard way — not with speeches, but with quiet, relentless action when we had almost nothing.

She showed me how to share when there was barely enough for us, and how to stand back up when life knocked us flat.

Reality Check exists because of her; every act of kindness and every honest conversation carries her fingerprints.

When this project reaches someone on the edge, it’s her strength and love moving through every word and choice.

Chef George

Chef George shaped how I see service. In the kitchen, he taught precision, timing, and respect for every plate that left the pass.

Outside the kitchen, he reminded me that people are always more important than performance or perfection.

The standard he set — show up, do it right, and care about the person in front of you — lives inside Reality Check.

His influence is baked into every detail, from how we speak to how we show up for those barely hanging on.

What Reality Check Stands For

Reality Check is built on lived experience — homelessness, service, disability, and decades of showing up for people in crisis with nothing but honesty and grit.

The mission is simple: help communities tell real from fake, protect the vulnerable from exploitation, and rebuild trust one transparent interaction at a time.
