Our Mission

Reality Check helps people make safer decisions with honesty, compassion, and lived experience. We provide transparency, trust scores, red flags, green flags, and simple explanations anyone can understand.

How Reality Check Works

  • Submit a message, profile, or situation.
  • We analyze red flags, green flags, and trust signals.
  • You receive a simple, human-readable explanation.
  • Final recommendation: Safe to trust, Be careful, or Do not trust.
