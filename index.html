Our Mission

Reality Check is a service built from lived experience — homelessness, military service, crisis support, and a lifetime of helping others. We provide transparent trust scores, real‑world guidance, and emotional grounding for people who feel overwhelmed by misinformation, scams, and uncertainty.

Every tool, every message, every page is designed to help someone who needs clarity, safety, and a real human connection.

Meet Charlie

Charlie is more than a mascot — he’s the emotional anchor of Reality Check. His presence reminds supporters that compassion, warmth, and connection are at the heart of everything we do.
