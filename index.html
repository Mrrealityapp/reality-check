Reality Check: Is This Safe to Trust?

Reality Check is a free service built to protect people from scams, manipulation, pressure tactics, and emotional exploitation. We analyze messages, profiles, requests, and claims using clear rules, lived experience, and pattern detection to help you make safe decisions with confidence and clarity.

What Reality Check Does

We analyze messages, profiles, requests, and claims for red flags, manipulation, inconsistencies, and pressure tactics. We identify safe signals, dangerous patterns, and missing information. You receive a clear verdict—Safe, Caution, or Do Not Trust—along with reasons you can understand and act on.

How It Works

Step 1: Submit a link, message, or description. Step 2: We analyze it using rules, experience, and pattern detection. Step 3: You receive a clear verdict with explanations so you can make safe, confident decisions.

Who We Help

We support anyone facing uncertainty, pressure, manipulation, or unclear requests. Seniors, veterans, single parents, vulnerable individuals, and anyone who needs clarity before sending money or personal information can rely on Reality Check for guidance.
