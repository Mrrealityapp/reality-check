Our Mission

Reality Check exists to restore trust, dignity, and safety in a world where misinformation harms real people every day. We help individuals verify what’s real, protect who matters, and make informed decisions with confidence.

Why Your Support Matters

Every contribution strengthens Reality Check’s ability to protect people from misinformation, scams, and digital harm. Your support isn’t charity — it’s empowerment. It helps individuals verify what’s real, make safer choices, and regain control in a confusing world.

When you give, you’re not just helping someone else. You’re helping build a safer, more trustworthy digital environment for everyone — including yourself.

Support the Mission

Our Transparency Promise

Reality Check is built on honesty. We believe donors deserve to know exactly how their support is used, what impact it creates, and how it protects real people from digital harm.

We publish clear, human-readable explanations of our work, our costs, and our mission — no jargon, no confusion, no hidden agendas. Trust is earned through clarity, and we are committed to earning it every day.

Meet Charlie

Charlie is more than a mascot — he’s a reminder of why compassion, clarity, and protection matter. His calm presence represents the heart of Reality Check: helping people feel safe, supported, and grounded in truth.

Every time you see Charlie, remember that our mission is human. Real people. Real lives. Real impact.
