What Reality Check Does

Reality Check helps you make safer decisions in a world full of half-truths, manipulation, and hidden agendas. You bring the situation; I bring lived experience, pattern recognition, and plain-language explanations.

You get clear red flags, green flags, and a grounded reality check so you can move forward with more confidence, or walk away before it costs you more than you can afford.

Why Your Support Matters

Reality Check is built on lived experience, not corporate funding. I’m a disabled veteran on a fixed income, showing up for people who are scared, confused, or one bad decision away from losing everything.

Your support doesn’t just “help a project” — it keeps a real human available to answer real cries for help, and it tells people in crisis that someone still cares enough to pick up.
