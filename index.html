Our Mission

Reality Check exists to restore trust, dignity, and real human connection in a world that often feels cold and automated. I’ve been homeless, I’ve been hungry, and I’ve been the person on the other end of the phone when someone had nowhere else to turn.

This project is a lifeline for people who are slipping through the cracks—homeless individuals, homeless veterans, and animals in need—through direct support, honest communication, and partnerships with organizations that actually show up.

Your support doesn’t disappear into a black box. It becomes food, warmth, safety, and a real person answering when someone reaches out for help.
