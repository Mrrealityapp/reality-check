Our Mission

Reality Check exists to help people in crisis make safer decisions with honesty, compassion, and transparency. Built on lived experience — homelessness, trauma, service, survival, and rebuilding — this project gives people what I never had: truth, clarity, and someone who genuinely cares.

Charlie

Charlie is more than a mascot — he’s a symbol of comfort, stability, and hope. A reminder that even in the darkest moments, connection can save a life.

What We Do

We provide:

  • Transparent trust scores
  • Red flags and green flags
  • Simple, human‑readable explanations
  • Guidance to avoid scams and manipulation
  • Support without judgment
  • Real stories from real experience

Why It Matters

People in crisis don’t need lectures. They need clarity. They need honesty. They need someone who understands what it feels like to be scared, alone, or unsure who to trust. Reality Check is built for them.
