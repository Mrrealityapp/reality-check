Reality Check

Restoring trust in a noisy world. Helping vulnerable people and animals get the support they need — with proof, transparency, and heart.

Why Reality Check exists
Real people. Real need. Real proof.
Radical transparency for people and animals in need

Reality Check was born from lived experience: trying to survive on a fixed income, caring for animals, and seeing how hard it is to know who to trust online. Fake profiles and staged stories make it scary to give — even when your heart wants to help.

This project uses verification, receipts, and clear updates so donors can see where help is going. When you support Reality Check, you’re not just helping one person. You’re helping build a safer way to give, especially for those who usually get ignored.

How Reality Check Works
Simple, honest, transparent support.
  • You see the real situation: I share honest updates about my life, my needs, and Charlie’s care — with photos, receipts, and proof when appropriate.
  • You choose how to help: Donate through PayPal or share the project. Every bit helps stabilize life here and expand support to others in need.
  • You see where your help goes: I post updates, receipts, and real outcomes so you always know what your support accomplished.
  • You help build a safer way to give: Your support helps create a model for verifying real people in need and protecting donors from scams.
Support the mission
Every bit of support keeps people and animals safer.

Your support helps cover essentials (housing, food, care for Charlie), outreach to others in crisis, and the tools needed to verify stories and share honest updates. No tricks. No vague “admin fees.”
