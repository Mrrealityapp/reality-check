Support That Protects You Too

Why Reality Check exists
Real people. Real need. Real proof.
Radical transparency for people and animals in need

Reality Check was born from lived experience: trying to survive on a fixed income, caring for animals, and seeing how hard it is to know who to trust online. Fake profiles and staged stories make it scary to give — even when your heart wants to help.

This project uses verification, receipts, and clear updates so donors can see where help is going. When you support Reality Check, you’re not just helping one person. You’re helping build a safer way to give, especially for those who usually get ignored.

How Reality Check Works
Simple, honest, transparent support.
  • You see the real situation: I share honest updates about my life, my needs, and Charlie’s care — with photos, receipts, and proof when appropriate.
  • You choose how to help: Donate through PayPal or share the project. Every bit helps stabilize life here and expand support to others in need.
  • You see where your help goes: I post updates, receipts, and real outcomes so you always know what your support accomplished.
  • You help build a safer way to give: Your support helps create a model for verifying real people in need and protecting donors from scams.
Why You Can Trust Reality Check
Radical transparency is the foundation of this project.
  • Real identity, real life: I’m a disabled veteran living on a fixed income in New Hampshire. This project is built from lived experience, not marketing.
  • Nothing hidden: I share updates, receipts, and proof of how support is used. No vague “admin fees,” no mystery spending.
  • Charlie is real too: My cat Charlie is part of this journey — his care, safety, and wellbeing are part of what supporters help sustain.
  • No team, no corporation: Reality Check is run by one person with a mission to build trust, not profit.
  • Every dollar matters: Support goes directly to survival, stability, and expanding this model to help others.
Meet Chris & Charlie
The human and the heart behind Reality Check.

Chris: I’m a disabled veteran working from home, doing everything I can to stay stable, stay honest, and build something that helps others. Life hasn’t been easy, but I’ve learned that transparency and compassion can change everything.

Charlie: Charlie is my daily companion, emotional anchor, and the furry heartbeat of this project. He’s a rescue cat with a huge personality, and supporters often say he’s the soul of Reality Check.

Together, we’re building a small but powerful mission: to show the world what honesty looks like — and to help others who are struggling do the same.
