Welcome to Reality Check

Reality Check is a clarity tool built from lived experience — designed to help people who feel overwhelmed, confused, pressured, or unsure who to trust. This project exists because no one should face manipulation, fear, or uncertainty alone.

Charlie — The Heart of Reality Check

Charlie is more than a mascot. He is comfort, stability, and the emotional anchor of this mission. His presence reminds us that connection saves lives, and that everyone deserves safety and clarity.

What We Do

We help people understand situations that feel confusing or unsafe. Reality Check breaks down behavior, motives, patterns, and emotional pressure so you can see the truth clearly — without judgment, shame, or fear.

Why It Matters

When you're overwhelmed, your brain can't process red flags. When you're scared, you can't see the truth. Reality Check gives you clarity when you need it most — so you can protect yourself, make grounded decisions, and stay safe.
