Meet Charlie

Charlie is more than a cat — he’s the heartbeat of Reality Check, the quiet presence that kept me going when everything else fell apart. His calm eyes and steady company turned long nights of doubt into something survivable.

When you see Charlie on this page, you’re seeing the reminder that someone out there still cares, still shows up, still stays. That’s what Reality Check is built to be for people who feel forgotten.

Me & Mama

My mother taught me compassion the hard way — not with speeches, but with quiet, relentless action. She showed me how to share when there was almost nothing to share, and how to stand back up when life knocked us flat.

Reality Check exists because of her. Every act of kindness, every honest conversation, every person we reach is a continuation of the love she poured into me when we had no safety net at all.

Chef George

Chef George shaped how I see service. In the kitchen, he taught precision, timing, and respect for every plate that left the pass. Outside the kitchen, he taught that people are always more important than the performance.

The standard he set — show up, do it right, care about the person in front of you — is baked into Reality Check. His influence lives in every detail, from how we speak to how we show up for those who are barely hanging on.

What Reality Check Stands For

Reality Check is built on lived experience — homelessness, service, disability, and decades of showing up for people in crisis with nothing but honesty and grit. No corporate spin, no polished distance, just the truth of what it’s like to survive when the system looks away.

The mission is simple: help communities tell real from fake, protect the vulnerable from exploitation, and rebuild trust one transparent interaction at a time. If you’ve ever felt like the world was lying to you, this space was built with you in mind.
