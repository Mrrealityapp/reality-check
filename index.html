Our Mission

Reality Check exists to restore trust, dignity, and hope — one real person at a time. We help those who are struggling, overlooked, or forgotten by offering compassion, connection, and real support rooted in lived experience.

About Reality Check

Reality Check was built from decades of service, hardship, and resilience. It is a lifeline created by someone who has lived the struggle — homelessness, trauma, loss, and rebuilding. This project is a promise: no one gets left behind.
