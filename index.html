Meet Charlie

Charlie is my daily companion and the steady heartbeat of Reality Check. His calm presence brings comfort to people who feel unseen or overwhelmed. He reminds us that connection can pull someone back from the edge. Charlie’s story proves that hope can come from the smallest moments. He inspires kindness, patience, and compassion in everyone he meets. And he shows that no one should ever have to struggle alone.

About Reality Check

Reality Check was created from lived experience — years of homelessness, service, and survival. This project exists to restore trust, dignity, and human connection in a world where too many people fall through the cracks.

I’ve spent decades serving others — in the military, in the Red Cross, in kitchens, behind bars, and on the streets helping people one-on-one. Every lesson, every hardship, every moment of grace shaped this mission.

With Charlie by my side, and with the memory of those who shaped me, Reality Check is here to help the homeless, the hungry, the forgotten, the veterans who feel invisible, and the animals who depend on us to speak for them.
