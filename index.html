Welcome to Reality Check

Reality Check is a clarity tool built from lived experience — designed to help people who feel overwhelmed, confused, pressured, or unsure who to trust. This project exists because no one should face manipulation, fear, or uncertainty alone.

Charlie — The Heart of Reality Check

Charlie is more than a mascot. He is comfort, stability, and the emotional anchor of this mission. His presence reminds us that connection saves lives, and that everyone deserves safety and clarity.

What We Do

We help people understand situations that feel confusing or unsafe. Reality Check breaks down behavior, motives, patterns, and emotional pressure so you can see the truth clearly — without judgment, shame, or fear.

Why It Matters

When you're overwhelmed, your brain can't process red flags. When you're scared, you can't see the truth. Reality Check gives you clarity when you need it most — so you can protect yourself, make grounded decisions, and stay safe.

How Reality Check Works — Full Breakdown

Step 1 — You Bring the Situation

A message, a conversation, a relationship, a request, a fear — anything.

Step 2 — We Break It Down

We analyze behavior, motives, pressure, inconsistencies, emotional hooks, and risk factors.

Step 3 — You Get a Trust Score

A clarity rating based on safety, transparency, consistency, motive, and risk.

Step 4 — Red Flags & Green Flags

We highlight danger signs and safety signs so you can see what’s really happening.

Step 5 — Plain-English Explanation

No jargon. No judgment. Just truth.

Step 6 — Your Next Steps

Clear guidance on what to ask, what to avoid, and how to protect yourself.

Step 7 — You Stay in Control

Reality Check never tells you what to do — it gives you clarity so YOU decide.

Who We Help

People who feel confused, overwhelmed, pressured, manipulated, isolated, or unsure who to trust. If you’ve ever felt lost or scared — this is for you.

Founder’s Story

Reality Check was built from decades of lived experience — homelessness, trauma, service, survival, and helping people in crisis. This project exists because I know what it feels like to have no clarity and no safety. Reality Check is the tool I wish I had when I needed it most.

Trust Score Explained

The Trust Score is not a number — it’s a clarity rating based on behavior, motive, consistency, transparency, emotional impact, and risk level.

Red Flags / Green Flags

Red Flags

  • Pressure
  • Urgency
  • Guilt
  • Confusion
  • Inconsistency
  • Secrecy
  • Money requests
  • Emotional manipulation

Green Flags

  • Transparency
  • Patience
  • Consistency
  • Respect
  • Stability
  • Clear communication
  • No pressure

Examples

Example 1 — Someone Asking for Money

Red flags: urgency, secrecy, emotional pressure. Trust Score: low.

Example 2 — A Relationship Moving Too Fast

Red flags: intensity, love bombing. Trust Score: medium.

Example 3 — A Friend Checking In

Green flags: consistency, care. Trust Score: high.
