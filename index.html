Our Mission

Reality Check exists to protect people from manipulation, scams, emotional abuse, and the kind of trust‑shattering moments that can destroy a life. I built this from lived experience — homelessness, service, crisis work, and survival. This isn’t corporate. This is human. This is real. This is needed.

For Mama

Mama taught me that you never walk past someone who is hurting and pretend you didn’t see them. She loved loud, forgave fast, and believed the smallest kindness could change the course of a life. Every plate I filled, every bar I worked, every night I stayed up with a stranger in crisis started with her voice in my head saying, “You don’t leave people behind.” Reality Check exists because Mama refused to look away, and now I won’t either.

For Chef George

Chef George took a homeless kid with rough edges and treated me like I belonged in a five‑star kitchen. He didn’t just teach technique; he taught discipline, pride, and how service can be a love language. When I plate a meal or build a system that protects people in crisis, his standards are in every detail. He showed me that excellence and compassion are not opposites — they’re the same work done all the way through.

Reality Check is built on lived experience, not corporate polish. I’m a disabled veteran on a fixed income, doing this so nobody has to go through the worst nights alone. Every dollar is accounted for. Every story is real. No games. No bullshit. Just help.
