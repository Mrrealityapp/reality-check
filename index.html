Reality Check: Is This Safe to Trust?

Reality Check protects people from scams, manipulation, pressure tactics, and emotional exploitation. We analyze messages, profiles, and requests using lived experience and pattern detection to help you make safe, confident decisions.

What Reality Check Does

We analyze messages, profiles, requests, and claims for red flags, manipulation, inconsistencies, and pressure tactics. You receive a clear verdict—Safe, Caution, or Do Not Trust—along with reasons you can act on.

How It Works

Step 1: Submit a link or message. Step 2: We analyze it using rules, experience, and pattern detection. Step 3: You receive a clear verdict with explanations so you can make safe decisions.

Who We Help

We support anyone facing uncertainty, pressure, manipulation, or unclear requests. Seniors, veterans, single parents, vulnerable individuals, and anyone who needs clarity before sending money or personal information can rely on Reality Check for guidance.
