Our Mission

Reality Check is a service built from lived experience — homelessness, military service, crisis support, and decades of helping people one‑on‑one. We exist to restore trust, provide clarity, and offer real help to those who feel invisible.

Every message, every design choice, every word is shaped by compassion, transparency, and the belief that no one should face crisis alone.

Support the Mission

Your support keeps Reality Check alive. It helps people in crisis, supports homeless individuals and veterans, and now extends to animals in need through partnerships with humane organizations.

Radical Transparency

Reality Check is built on honesty. No hidden agendas. No corporate polish. Just lived experience, clear communication, and full transparency about how support is used.

We believe trust is earned — and we show our work.
