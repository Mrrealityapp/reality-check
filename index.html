Mission

Reality Check is built on lived experience — homelessness, service, disability, and decades of showing up for people in crisis. The mission is simple: help communities tell real from fake, protect the vulnerable, and rebuild trust one honest interaction at a time.

Charlie the cat

Meet Charlie

Charlie is more than a companion — he’s the steady heartbeat behind Reality Check. His calm presence carried me through nights when everything felt impossible. He reminds me daily that even the smallest comfort can save someone’s spirit.

Charlie represents loyalty, grounding, and the quiet strength people need. This project carries his energy — steady, warm, and always present. Every supporter becomes part of that circle of calm and care. Charlie’s story is the foundation of this mission. And his presence continues to guide Reality Check forward.

Me & Mama

Mama taught me compassion through action, not words. She showed me how to give even when we had almost nothing. Her strength shaped the backbone of Reality Check. Every act of kindness here carries her influence.

She taught resilience through love and consistency. Her lessons guide every decision I make today. This project honors her living legacy. And her spirit is woven into every message we share.

Chef George

Chef George shaped my understanding of service. He taught precision, timing, and respect for every detail. He believed people mattered more than perfection. His lessons live inside Reality Check’s mission.

He showed me how to show up fully for others. His influence guides how we support those in need. This block honors his impact on my life. And his spirit continues to inspire this work.
