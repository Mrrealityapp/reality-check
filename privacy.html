Privacy Policy

Reality Check is built on transparency and trust. This page explains what information is collected, how it’s used, and how your privacy is protected.

What We Collect

Reality Check collects only the minimum information needed to operate the site and improve the experience. This may include basic analytics (like page visits), voluntary information you submit through forms, and data required for donation processing through trusted third‑party services such as PayPal.

How Your Information Is Used

Information is used solely to maintain the site, improve features, and support communication when you reach out. Reality Check does not sell, trade, or share your personal information with outside parties except when required to process donations or comply with the law.

Third‑Party Services

Donations are handled securely through PayPal. Any information you provide during a donation is processed according to PayPal’s own privacy policies. Reality Check does not store or have access to your financial details.

Your Choices

You can choose not to provide personal information, disable cookies in your browser, or contact Reality Check with questions about your data. This site is designed to respect your choices and minimize data collection wherever possible.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, you can reach out through the contact options provided on the site.
